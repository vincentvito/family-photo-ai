Holiday cards are one of the hardest family photo jobs because the image has to do several things at once. It should feel seasonal, flatter everyone, leave room for card layout, and still look believable.

AI can help, but the best results come from giving it a clear job. If you are starting from separate phone photos, read the full guide to using an [AI family photo generator for family portraits from separate photos](/blog/ai-family-photo-generator-family-portrait-from-separate-photos).

## Choose the card mood first

Before you upload photos, decide what kind of card you want. Cozy and candlelit will lead to a different result than bright studio minimalism.

Good directions include:

- Cozy winter morning at home.
- Classic studio holiday portrait.
- Elegant black tie family card.
- Playful matching pajamas scene.
- Snowy outdoor portrait with warm light.

## Avoid overloading the scene

Seasonal does not need to mean crowded. A few visual cues are usually enough: soft lights, winter textures, greenery, a fireplace, or a clean card border.

Too many props can make faces compete with the background. Keep the family as the clear subject.

## Leave space for card design

If you plan to add text later, ask for negative space. A composition with room at the top, bottom, or one side is easier to turn into a card.

## Use current photos of kids

For children, use recent reference photos. Even a beautiful old photo can create a result that feels slightly wrong if the child has changed a lot since then.

## Make a small batch

Generate a few options in the same style and compare them like card proofs. The best holiday card image usually has one simple quality: it feels like a family you recognize at a glance.
