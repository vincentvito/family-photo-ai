# FamilyShoot Father’s Day gift-guide and directory pack

Prepared for Matteo’s approved growth item #2 on 2026-06-04. No submissions, outreach, paid placements, or external messages have been sent.

## Universal submission copy

### Short description

FamilyShoot helps families create personalized family portraits and cards from their favorite photos. It is a thoughtful Father’s Day gift for dads, grandpas, long-distance families, and anyone who wants a family portrait that feels personal.

### Title options

- Personalized Father’s Day Family Portraits
- FamilyShoot: Custom Family Portrait Gifts for Dad
- Create a Father’s Day Portrait From Your Family Photos

### Long submission copy

FamilyShoot is a personalized family portrait service for people who want a meaningful photo gift without organizing a full photoshoot. Families can use their favorite everyday photos to create polished portraits and cards for Father’s Day, grandparents, reunions, anniversaries, and long-distance families. It works especially well when dad or grandpa is usually behind the camera, missing from the best photos, or deserves a keepsake the family can print or save.

### Tags / categories

Father’s Day gifts, personalized gifts, photo gifts, family portraits, gifts for dad, gifts for grandpa, family cards

### Required asset checklist

- Logo/avatar
- Live Father’s Day URL: https://familyshoot.com/fathers-day
- 2 to 3 product/gallery images
- 1 Father’s Day visual
- Pricing/package summary if requested
- Founder/maker contact email
- Short and long descriptions
- Clear disclosure that images are AI-generated/personalized
