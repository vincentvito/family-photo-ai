# FamilyShoot Seasonal Hero Campaign

Board task: Marketing 02 - Create seasonal hero campaign offer
Date: 2026-05-09

## First Campaign Wedge

Holiday cards from photos you already have.

This is the sharpest first wedge because it has urgency, a familiar buyer, and a clear before/after problem: families want a polished card, but they do not have one good recent photo of everyone together.

## Landing Page Hero Copy

Headline:
Create your holiday card without booking a photoshoot.

Subhead:
Upload everyday photos of each family member and FamilyShoot turns them into a polished, card-ready portrait in minutes.

Primary CTA:
Make my holiday card

Secondary CTA:
See card styles

Trust line:
No studio day, no matching outfits, no chasing everyone into the same frame.

Short offer strip:
Start with a Single Keepsake, or make a few options with a Three-pack or Eight-pack.

## Alternate Hero Angles

1. The family card you meant to take, made from the photos you already have.
2. Turn scattered phone photos into one polished holiday card.
3. Everyone in the card, even if everyone was never in the same photo.
4. Skip the photoshoot. Keep the family portrait.
5. A holiday card for the year you almost forgot to take family photos.

## Five Short Hooks

1. We did not have one good family photo, so we made the card from the photos we had.
2. Grandma wanted a family portrait. Nobody had time for a photoshoot.
3. Your holiday card does not need a perfect photo day.
4. Turn separate phone photos into one card-ready family portrait.
5. The easiest holiday card is the one made from pictures already on your phone.

## Offer And Pricing Copy

Single Keepsake:
One finished family portrait or card. Best for a quick gift, a holiday card, or testing one look.

Three-pack:
Three finished portraits or cards. Best for comparing styles, choosing a card design, or making options for grandparents.

Eight-pack:
Eight finished portraits or cards. Best for holiday cards, multiple relatives, and a few frame-worthy variations.

Short pricing section intro:
Choose one finished portrait, a few card options, or a bigger set for gifts and keepsakes.

Checkout reassurance:
Upload only the photos needed for your shoot. FamilyShoot keeps generated images and references for a limited time based on your plan.
